# Holistic Investment Terminal

A working, AI-assisted **company analysis and investment decision terminal** for Indian listed equities. It helps an
investor reach a BUY / BUY BELOW / WAIT / WATCHLIST / HOLD / REDUCE / SELL-AVOID / INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE decision by
combining objective evidence, forward-looking scenarios, subjective judgment, and investor-specific suitability.

> *Understand the business. Challenge the thesis. Value the future. Protect the downside. Know yourself before investing.*

This is decision support, not investment advice. It never claims to predict prices.

---

## Quick start

```bash
cd investment-terminal
npm install
npm start            # http://127.0.0.1:3000
npm test             # 36 unit + integration tests
```

Requires **Node.js ≥ 22.5** (uses the built-in `node:sqlite`). Create an account in the browser. Each new account gets
four **fictional, clearly-labelled sample companies** so you can try the whole workflow straight away:

| Sample (fictional) | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Aravalli Consumer Brands (SAMPLE) | A great business priced for perfection. Quality ≈ 8/10, but the excessive-valuation override gives **WAIT**. |
| Deccan Precision Engineering (SAMPLE) | A quality industrial near its entry price. **BUY ONLY BELOW ₹556**, or **BUY** at a lower price once the self-assessment and red-team review are done. |
| Konkan Specialty Chemicals (SAMPLE) | A cyclical at peak margins, with promoter pledging, an emphasis-of-matter and missed guidance. **AVOID**. |
| Vindhya Infra & Realty (SAMPLE) | High debt, an auditor resignation and a SEBI finding. Governance and solvency overrides force **SELL / AVOID** with escalation. |

Sample figures are generated by `scripts/build-sample-data.js` (`npm run build:sample`). They are internally
consistent but **synthetic**, and every screen, report and export labels them as such.

### Configuration (environment variables, server-side only)

| Variable | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `PORT`, `HOST` | Listen address (default `127.0.0.1:3000`) |
| `DB_FILE` | SQLite path (default `data/terminal.db`) |
| `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` | Turns on the optional AI explanation, AI red-team and AI answer-grading layer (Claude, model `claude-opus-5-5`; override with `AI_MODEL`) |
| `MARKET_DATA_ENDPOINT`, `MARKET_DATA_API_KEY` | A **licensed** quote provider returning `{ price, timestamp }` for `GET ?symbol=TICKER`. There is no scraping fallback. |
| `COOKIE_SECURE=1` / `TRUST_PROXY=1` | Use these behind HTTPS or a reverse proxy |

API keys never reach the browser. **Settings → Integrations** shows the real status of every integration. Nothing
is labelled "live" unless it is.

## What it does (mapped to the build brief)

| Module | Where | Highlights |
|---|---|---|
| Input & research engine | Search, Documents | Create a company by name, ticker, ISIN, website or thesis. Upload CSV/XLSX statements (alias mapping; unrecognised rows are reported, never guessed) and PDF/TXT annual reports, transcripts, rating, broker and industry reports. The tool extracts auditor qualifications, guidance, pledges, RPTs, rating actions and capex with page references, and scores transcript tone. You confirm each finding before it is applied. |
| A. Business quality | Business model | Plain-language model, 10 scored quality items (each needs evidence), quantitative fingerprints, automatic classification (exceptional, average, cyclical, turnaround, speculative, declining). |
| B. Industry | Industry & peers | TAM, growth (3/5/10y), life-cycle, Porter's five forces, PESTLE, position, and a peer benchmarking matrix. The **investor knowledge test** lives in Module J. |
| C. Financials & forensics | Financials | 5–10 years of growth, profitability, balance sheet, cash flow and working capital, plus 16 forensic checks. Each check is labelled **confirmed** (a disclosed fact) or **warning** (a signal to investigate). |
| D. Management & governance | Management | Credibility tracker (promise → outcome → variance), governance record typed as allegation / disclosed / regulatory finding / proven, auditor events, shareholding and pledge history, capital-allocation metrics. |
| E. Moat | Moat | 15 moat sources scored with evidence, cross-checked against years of ROIC above the cost of capital. Shows trend, width, 3/5/10-year durability and the evidence that would disprove the moat. |
| F. Prospects & scenarios | Prospects & scenarios | Bear/base/bull sliders that recompute valuation, scores and the decision **instantly in the browser** (the same engine runs client- and server-side). Projections at 3, 5 and 10 years, and a catalyst register that separates announced, approved, executing and speculative items. |
| G. Risk register | Risk register | Probability × impact, warning signs, mitigation, residual risk, confidence. Thesis-breaker analysis. |
| H. Valuation | Valuation lab | P/E, EV/EBITDA, P/B, P/S, P/FCF, yields and PEG (with caveats). Methods are weighted by business type (DCF, historical band, peers, DDM, normalised earnings, P/B-ROE, NAV, SOTP), so DCF is not forced onto lenders or cyclicals. Also: reverse DCF, two sensitivity heatmaps, 3- and 5-year expected returns vs hurdle, index and fixed income, margin of safety and entry range. |
| I. Market expectations | Valuation lab, Overview | Reverse DCF (what growth the price implies), multiples vs history, transcript tone. Short-term timing is kept separate from long-term merit. |
| J. Investor self-knowledge | Self-assessment | Seven self-ratings plus seven **evidence questions** graded against the company's knowledge base (optionally by AI). Also conviction quality (evidence-backed vs emotional), suitability, 10 bias detectors with countermeasures, and an overconfidence flag. Scored independently of the company. |
| K. Portfolio fit | Portfolio fit, My portfolio | Sector allocation before/after, single-stock and sector limits, macro-factor overlap (a labelled correlation proxy), days to exit, a stress loss, and opportunity cost against your other ideas, an index fund and fixed income. |
| L. Scoring engine | Holistic score | 10 weighted dimensions (default 12/10/12/10/10/12/14/8/6/6), adjustable live and savable. Each dimension shows score, weight, contribution, evidence, positives/negatives, confidence, missing data and what to monitor. Risks are not double-counted. Unscored dimensions sit at a neutral 5. |
| Overrides | Holistic score, Report | Serious governance, insolvency, unreliable or stale data, excessive valuation, a broken thesis, poor investor understanding, short horizon, concentration, and any critical (<3/10) dimension or near-certain catastrophic risk. These **cap** the decision whatever the score. |
| M. Decision report | Decision report | Executive dashboard, decision with all 10 explanations, entry range, holder vs buyer view, BUY checklist and decision trace. Exports: **PDF, HTML, CSV**, plus a comparison CSV. |
| Red team | Decision report | A rule-based strongest case *against* the investment, with disconfirming tests (plus optional AI red team). A BUY stays **provisional** until you answer each point. |
| N. Monitoring & alerts | Watchlist & alerts | After any evidence change, watchlisted companies are re-analysed and diffed. Critical/High/Medium/Low alerts cover score drops, new forensic flags, pledges, promoter selling, rating changes, thesis breaks, entry-price triggers and announcements. Price moves alert only if you opt in. |
| Thesis tracker | Thesis tracker | Versioned thesis with measurable assumptions (metric/operator/threshold) and sell triggers, evaluated live. Stores the valuation and risks recorded at creation. |
| History | Decision history | Recorded decisions with the snapshot from that time, return since, score and value then vs now, and a process-vs-outcome review. |
| Compare | Compare | 2–8 companies side by side, ranked under the same weights, rates and rules. |

### Why a BUY is never produced from the score alone

`engine/decision.js` builds a base decision from **business quality** (a composite of six dimensions) and **valuation
status**. Override caps then apply. A BUY requires every item of the BUY checklist:

- quality ≥ 6.5
- margin of safety ≥ the required level
- holistic score ≥ 65
- no critical override
- completed self-assessment with knowledge ≥ 4
- data confidence ≥ 50%

It also needs a red-team review before it becomes final. This is how the tool separates "a great business" from "a
great investment at today's price".

## Architecture

```
engine/            Pure ES modules (run on server AND in the browser)
  financials.js    statement normalisation & ratios      forensics.js   accounting checks
  scenarios.js     bear/base/bull projections            valuation.js   methods, reverse DCF, sensitivity
  scoring.js       10 dimension scorers + aggregation    decision.js    decision ladder + override rules
  investor.js      Module J scoring & bias detection     portfolio.js   fit & opportunity cost
  redteam.js       counter-thesis generator              monitoring.js  thesis evaluation & alerts
  dataquality.js   provenance, staleness, confidence     analyze.js     orchestrator + narrative
server/
  app.js / http.js HTTP server, CSRF/origin checks, security headers, static files
  api.js           REST API (auth, companies, analysis, documents, thesis, decisions, watchlist, alerts, exports)
  auth.js          scrypt hashing, hashed session tokens, HttpOnly SameSite=Strict cookies, rate limiting
  db.js            SQLite schema (users, companies, documents, analyses, assessments, theses, decisions,
                   watchlist, alerts, holdings, settings, red-team reviews, audit_log)
  ingest.js        CSV/XLSX/PDF/TXT parsing & signal extraction
  report.js        HTML / PDF / CSV generation
  adapters/        integration registry + licensed market-data adapter
  ai/claude.js     optional AI layer (grounded strictly in computed evidence; output labelled AI-generated)
public/            Vanilla-JS SPA (20+ screens), light/dark themes, responsive; Chart.js served locally
data/sample/       fictional sample companies        test/   node:test unit + integration suites
```

Provenance is shown everywhere with these labels: *Reported*, *Uploaded*, *Sample*, *Calculated*, *Assumption*,
*Judgment*, *AI estimate*. Every dataset carries an as-of date. A financial year older than 15 months, or a price
older than 7 days, is flagged stale and lowers confidence.

## Security

- Passwords are hashed with scrypt.
- Session tokens are random, stored as SHA-256 hashes, and sent in HttpOnly SameSite=Strict cookies.
- Every mutation needs a same-origin request and the `X-Requested-With` header.
- Strict CSP, `frame-ancestors 'none'`, nosniff.
- Login and registration are rate-limited.
- Upload size is capped; editable fields are whitelisted; inputs are validated.
- Every user's data is isolated.
- Static serving is protected against path traversal.
- CSV exports neutralise formula injection.
- Every mutation is written to the audit log (Settings → Audit log).

## Known limitations (honest status)

- **No live data feeds are bundled.** Live NSE/BSE filings, shareholding, prices and news need licensed APIs, which
  are not configured. Use manual upload with dates, or point `MARKET_DATA_ENDPOINT` at a licensed provider.
- PDF extraction reads the text layer only. Scanned PDFs are flagged; there is no OCR. Tables in PDFs are not turned
  into statements — upload those as CSV/XLSX.
- Answers to the knowledge test are graded by concept coverage against a knowledge base. AI grading is optional.
- Correlation is a factor-overlap proxy, not a calculation from price returns.
- Scores are transparent heuristics. Valuation outputs are estimates with wide uncertainty.
